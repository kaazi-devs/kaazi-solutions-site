FAQ

Questions, answered.

The stuff you'd ask on the first call — addressed up front.

What kinds of problems do you typically solve?

Mostly the kind that scale poorly with humans — repetitive ops, complex routing, decisions made at high volume. Anywhere a smart system outperforms a team by an order of magnitude is where we live.

How long does a typical engagement take?

Discovery runs 1–2 weeks. Build typically takes 4–12 weeks depending on scope. Most clients see their first agent in production within 90 days of the first conversation.

Do we need to know AI to work with you?

No. We translate. You bring the problem and the business context; we bring the technical strategy and explain every step in plain language. Most of our clients aren't AI engineers — they're operators trying to remove friction from the way work actually gets done.

How do you handle data privacy and security?

Our agents can run in your infrastructure or in dedicated environments — your data never leaves your control. We don't train on your data, and we sign your NDA, not ours.

What if AI isn't actually the right answer?

Then we say so. Discovery is paid but small, and if we can't see a clear path to outcome, we won't take the build. We're not in business to ship things we don't believe will work.

Can you integrate with our existing systems?

Almost always yes. We've built into Salesforce, SAP, legacy on-prem databases, modern SaaS, custom APIs — pretty much everything short of mainframes. The integration is part of the engineering, not a separate scope.

Custom Model Training

LLM Fine-Tuning & Custom Models

Generic AI knows everything. Your business needs AI that knows you. We fine-tune models on your data so they speak your domain natively.

What we fine-tune

  • Open-source models (Llama, Mistral, Qwen) for full ownership
  • Closed-source models via provider APIs when licensing makes sense
  • Q&A, classification, generation, and tool-use specializations

Our process

  • Data collection from your existing assets (docs, transcripts, structured data)
  • Synthetic data generation where coverage is thin
  • Iterative training runs with continuous evaluation
  • Hosted or self-hosted deployment with monitoring

Why fine-tune over prompts: When prompt engineering plateaus and you need an order of magnitude more specificity.

Common use cases: Legal, medical, finance, and technical-support assistants. Brand-voice writing models. Models that know your internal APIs and tools.
